# Third-party components and licensing

## Stockfish 19 / Stockfish.js 19

This app packages unmodified Stockfish 19 JavaScript/WebAssembly engine files supplied by the user:

- `engine/stockfish-19-lite-single.js` and `.wasm` — single-threaded Lite build
- `engine/stockfish-19.js` and `.wasm` — multithreaded Full build

The engines are GPL-3.0 licensed (see `LICENSE-GPL-3.0.txt`). Stockfish.js is maintained at <https://github.com/nmrugg/stockfish.js>, with Stockfish at <https://github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish>. Source code for the engine corresponding to these compiled distributions is obtainable from the Stockfish.js project and its corresponding release; consult its repository and distribution instructions. If redistributing this app, ensure the applicable GPL terms and corresponding-source obligations are met.

The educational website's interface, lesson explanations, and new exercises are independent material. Its content and visual design are not associated with or endorsed by Chess.com, Lichess, Stockfish, or The Steps Method.

## Rated puzzles

The positions, solution lines, puzzle ratings, opening classifications and themes in puzzle-bank.js come from the [Lichess open puzzle database](https://database.lichess.org/#puzzles), distributed under CC0. Remove the leading L from an entry’s ID to find the original puzzle at lichess.org/training/ID. Ratings describe puzzles in that database.
